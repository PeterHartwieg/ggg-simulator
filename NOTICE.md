# What the MIT licence covers

The [MIT licence](LICENSE) covers everything in this repository that the author wrote:

- the game code in `js/`, `css/`, `index.html` and `404.html`
- the game's own text and numbers in `js/data/`
- the statistics Worker in `worker/`
- the tools in `tools/`
- the hosting config and the generated `art/manifest.js`

| `art/ggg/` | Grinding Gear Games | Game artwork, logos and loading screens. Each file's source is listed in `art/credits.html` and `art/sources.json`. |
| `art/history/` | Grinding Gear Games, unless `art/credits.html` names someone else | Historic screenshots and photos. Each one's source and owner is listed in `art/credits.html`. |
