# Grinding Gear Games / Path of Exile 1 + 2: dated history, 2006 to 2026-11-11

Research compiled 2026-11-11 for a fan-made "GGG simulator" tycoon game. Web research only.

## Conventions

- `[unverified]` means I could not confirm the item in a source fetched during this session. Most of these are well-known facts written from general knowledge; treat them as leads to check, not as settled.
- Money is NZ$ unless marked US$.

  - **Steam** = monthly peak for the launch month from steamcharts (hourly samples, so slightly below SteamDB). Steam data starts October 2013.
  - **All-platform** = GGG's own announced concurrency (PC standalone client + Steam, later consoles). GGG has said Steam is roughly 60% of PoE1 players.

## 1. People (public information only)

### Founders

- **Chris Wilson**: co-founder, managing director/CEO and the public face of the studio. Lead designer and producer of PoE1; fronted almost every league reveal until 2023. University of Auckland computer science graduate (also a commerce degree). Paid for early development from his earnings in a software-security job. Aged 31 in December 2013. Gave the GDC 2019 talk "Designing Path of Exile to Be Played Forever". His own site says he stepped down at the end of 2023 after 17 years; the Companies Office lists him as ceasing as managing director on 2025-01-21; NZ Herald says he resigned as a director in February 2025. Founded **Light Pattern** in 2025 (small NZ studio, unannounced small game, about 10 staff by mid-2026, described as years away). Runs a YouTube channel on game design; trustee of a charity (KeaCoding) teaching programming in Auckland schools. Sources: https://www.chriswilson.gg/ , `GD25`, `NZH25`, `NR17`, https://www.nzherald.co.nz/business/business-leader-of-the-year-finalist-2013-chris-wilson/2YQKK63UXFF2GWOBYYQJCAUCI4/
- **Jonathan Rogers**: co-founder, technical director and lead programmer; met Wilson studying computer science at the University of Auckland. Built the in-house engine `[unverified detail]`. Game director of PoE2. Joined the board and became CEO/managing director in February 2025. In 2025 funded a STEM-focused private school with physics teacher Tristan O'Hanlon. Sources: `WP-GGG`, `NZH25`, https://www.rnz.co.nz/national/programmes/ninetonoon/audio/2018998125/game-developer-and-physics-teacher-to-make-stem-school
- **Erik Olofsson**: co-founder, art director / creative director. A Swede Wilson met online (PC Gamer reports Wilson met one co-founder playing Diablo II online); flew to NZ a few months after founding. Left around 2022 to run **Erik's Curiosa**, maker of the card game *Sorcery: Contested Realm* (Kickstarter March 2022: NZ$5.78m from 6,456 backers). Reportedly still consults for GGG `[unverified]`. Sources: `WP-GGG`, `NZH25`, https://fextralife.com/sorcery-contested-realm-an-upcoming-classic-tradable-card-game-smashes-kickstarter-goals/ , https://www.pcgamer.com/the-making-of-path-of-exile/ (not fetchable; quoted via search summary)
- **Brian Weissman**: described himself in 2012 as the fourth co-founder; based in Seattle; credited as executive producer and writer. Famous in Magic: The Gathering as creator of "The Deck". Source: https://www.toptiertactics.com/13179/path-of-exile-interview-with-grinding-gear-games-brian-weissman/ , `WP-GGG`

## 2. Chronological timeline

### 2006 to 2009: the garage years

- **2006**: Chris Wilson and Jonathan Rogers, friends from University of Auckland computer science, found Grinding Gear Games to build an online action RPG in the Diablo II mould. Work starts in Wilson's bedroom, then his garage in **New Lynn**, West Auckland (the garage was not in Titirangi; Titirangi was the first office). Erik Olofsson flies in from Sweden a few months later. Brian Weissman contributes from Seattle. Game hook: starting state, 3 to 5 people, zero revenue. Sources: `WP-GGG`, `NZH18`, https://hardcoregamer.com/features/interviews/grinding-gears-about-path-of-exile-with-chris-wilson/35504/
- **2006 to 2010**: about four years of deliberately secret development. Funding is Wilson's security-job earnings, the founders' own money and some private investors in the US. The team has no contact with established NZ studios and frugality shapes the first version. Game hook: long cash-burn phase with no income. Sources: `NR17`, `RNZ12`, PC Gamer feature (via search summary), https://futurefive.co.nz/story/kiwi-developer-presents-path-of-exile-at-penny-arcade-expo
- **Late 2008**: move from the garage to a first office in **Titirangi**. Game hook: first office upgrade. Source: `NZH18`

### 2010 to 2012: announcement, closed beta, the supporter packs

- **About June 2010**: alpha begins. In 2010 the team runs a local server for friends and family; seeing them trade and grind convinces Wilson the game works. Sources: `WP-PoE`, `SPIN23`
- **2010-09-01**: Path of Exile announced publicly (free-to-play, PC, planned for 2011) with trailers and screenshots. The website gets only about 2,000 views that day despite press coverage. Game hook: reveal event with underwhelming hype. Sources: https://worthplaying.com/article/2010/9/1/news/76614-path-of-exile-announced-screens-trailer , `ESC20`
- **September 2010**: shown at PAX in Seattle; journalists play it. Source: https://futurefive.co.nz/story/kiwi-developer-presents-path-of-exile-at-penny-arcade-expo
- **August 2011**: closed beta begins (version 0.9.0) with two acts, no microtransactions and no trade screen. Invites trickle out through a website "beta key timer" that picks a random registrant every interval, to avoid swamping servers. Game hook: drip-feed invite mechanic. Sources: `R1`, `WP-PoE`
- **2012-03-30 to 04-01**: first public stress-test weekend. Source: https://www.dsogaming.com/news/path-of-exile-public-stress-test-weekend-starts-in-march-30th/
- **2012-04-04**: **closed beta supporter packs** go on sale: pre-purchased microtransaction points plus instant beta access. Tiers: Early Access US$10, Closed Beta US$15, Kiwi US$25 (kiwi pet), Bronze US$50, Silver US$100, Gold US$250, and Diamond (US$1,000 `[price unverified]`; Diamond supporters got to design a unique item). Game hook: the crowdfunding decision that saved the studio. Sources: https://poedb.tw/us/Closed_Beta_supporter_pack , `R1`
- **2012-04-10**: more than US$245,000 raised in the first six days (some outlets rounded to US$200,000). Source: https://web.archive.org/web/20120505001148/http://pcworld.co.nz/pcworld/pcw.nsf/news/nz-game-path-of-exile-crowd-funds-245000-in-six-days
- **2012-05-11 to 05-13**: second public weekend, debuting the Shadow class, ending two days before Diablo III's launch. Game hook: marketing timed against a giant competitor. Source: https://www.tentonhammer.com/news/stress-test-path-of-exile-this-weekend
- **2012-05-15**: Diablo III launches with always-online server errors and a real-money auction house `[unverified, general knowledge]`. PoE's no-pay-to-win, tradeable-orbs pitch positions it as the alternative for disappointed Diablo II fans. A direct, sourced statement from GGG about player inflow was not found.
- **2012-06-19**: RNZ profile: 18 staff, about NZ$2m spent over five and a half years, US$700,000 taken in ten weeks of paid beta from 385,000 accounts, the company now breaking even on staff costs, launch planned for August 2012 (it slipped to January 2013). Game hook: the studio went from pure burn to break-even on pack sales alone. The often-told "nearly out of money" version of this story is `[unverified]` as a direct quote. Source: `RNZ12`
- **2012-07-27 to 07-29**: third stress-test weekend, preparing for open beta. Source: https://www.theouterhaven.net/2012/07/path-of-exile-open-beta-from-july-27th-july-29th
- **2012**: studio at about 18 people is outgrowing the Titirangi office. Source: toptiertactics interview (link in People)

### 2013: open beta and 1.0

- **2013-01-21**: crowdfunding total passes US$2.2m. Source: `WP-PoE`
- **2013-01-23**: **open beta (0.10.0)**: free to play, last-ever wipe, first half of Act 3, first cosmetic store. Closed beta packs end the same day having raised about US$2.5m over 35 weeks, including about US$200,000 of Kiwi packs in the last two days. About 50,000 people try to download the 1.5 GB patch at once and choke the CDN; the realm crashes several times; peak concurrency 56,700 the day after launch, later reported as 69,850 before the realm fell over; a login bug briefly forces a shutdown; thousands of support tickets pile up and developers answer them. Launch-day web traffic equals the entire first year after announcement. Game hook: first big server-capacity crisis. Sources: `GGG:73903`, https://www.pcgamesn.com/path-exile-open-beta-demand-overloads-grinding-gear-games-servers , https://www.pcgamer.com/path-of-exile-open-beta-player-count/
- **2013-03-15**: 2 million registered players. Source: `WP-PoE`
- **Late March 2013**: 22 developers; Wilson expects 50% growth within the year and says monetisation is strong because the model is not predatory. Source: Hardcore Gamer interview (link above)
- **2013-06-05 / 06-07**: patch 0.11.0 ("open beta phase two") and the first challenge leagues, **Anarchy** and **Onslaught**, running four months to 1.0. 61 players complete all eight challenges. Open beta supporter packs go on sale the same day. Game hook: invention of the league model. Sources: https://www.pcgamer.com/path-of-exile-update-0-11-0-brings-new-leagues-of-onslaught-and-anarchy , `PDB`, https://poedb.tw/us/Supporter_pack
- **2013-10-23**: **version 1.0.0** and the **Steam launch**. Second half of Act 3 (Dominus), the Scion class, guilds, PvP, trigger gems; leagues Domination and Nemesis. Steam peak 34,371 (`SC1`); Wilson later put the all-platform launch peak at just under 70,000 (`ESC20`). Sources: `R1`, `WP-PoE`
- **November 2013**: publishing deal with **Garena** for Southeast Asia; Singapore/Malaysia closed beta from 2013-11-14; Taiwan, Thailand and CIS realms followed (dates `[unverified]`; the CIS realm closed in 2016; Garena's Taiwan realm was later shut and migrated to Hotcool, date `[unverified]`). Game hook: regional publisher choice. Sources: https://www.capsulecomputers.com.au/2013/11/grinding-gear-games-partners-with-garena-online-to-bring-hit-arpg-path-of-exile-to-asia/ , `GGG:3494210`
- **December 2013**: 4 million registered users and 250,000 daily players (2013-12-02); GameSpot PC Game of the Year; IGN best PC RPG; staff about 55 (up from 22 in January); the company is profitable, which Wilson calls a big relief; Wilson is an NZ Herald Business Leader of the Year finalist. Sources: https://www.gamebanshee.com/kpk4k , NZ Herald 2013 (link in People), `SPIN23`

### 2014 to 2016: finding the 13-week rhythm

- **February 2014**: 5 million registered players, more than NZ's population. Source: `WP-PoE`, `SPIN23`
- **2014-03-05**: **Sacrifice of the Vaal** (1.1.0): Atziri, corrupted areas, Vaal Orbs; leagues Ambush and Invasion. Steam peak 19,489. Sources: `R1`, `SC1`
- **2014-03-18**: Blizzard shuts Diablo III's auction houses `[unverified, general knowledge]`; Reaper of Souls follows a week later and wins players back.
- **2014**: studio photographed at a **Henderson** office (the exact move date from Titirangi is `[unverified]`; it had certainly happened by April 2017). Source: `NR17`, search summary of NZME caption
- **2014-08-20**: **Forsaken Masters** (1.2.0): seven masters, hideouts, crafting benches; leagues Rampage and Beyond. GGG later said its then-huge budget is about a week's spend by 2018 standards. Steam peak 24,493. Sources: `R1`, `SC1`
- **2014-12-12**: 1.3.0: Torment and Bloodlines leagues, PvP overhaul, invitational tournaments on a short-lived Twitch channel. Steam peak 13,689, the lowest launch on record. Game hook: a trough before the 2.0 recovery. Sources: `R1`, `SC1`
- **2015 (about)**: deal with **Tencent** to publish in China (GGG said in August 2017 that it had been working with Tencent for two years). Sources: https://gamesbeat.com/tencent-is-bringing-path-of-exile-to-china/ , `GGG:1970529`
- **2015-07-10**: **The Awakening** (2.0.0): Act 4, jewels, divination cards, and lockstep networking, which GGG credits with fixing the game's notorious desync. Last split softcore/hardcore leagues (Warbands, Tempest). Steam peak 32,284. Game hook: paying down technical debt as an expansion feature. Sources: `R2`, `SC1`
- **2015-12-11**: **Talisman** (2.1.0): released alone because Ascendancy needed three more months. First single shared league, first league boss, and the start of the **13-week cycle**. Wilson later said players leaving after each release was horrifying until one full cycle showed they came back. Game hook: the core loop of the tycoon game. Sources: `R2`, `SPIN23`
- **2016-03-04**: **Ascendancy** (2.2.0): the Labyrinth and 19 ascendancy classes; Perandus league, reworked a week before launch and one of the best-liked. Steam peak 37,266. Sources: `R2`, `SC1`
- **2016-06-03**: **Prophecy** (2.3.0). GGG's lesson: do a few things well, not too much at once. Source: `R2`
- **2016-09-02**: **Atlas of Worlds** (2.4.0): the Atlas endgame, 30 new maps; Essence league. Source: `R2`
- **2016-12-02**: **Breach** (2.5.0): a community hall-of-fame league; later leagues are judged by the "Breach test" for risk and reward. Steam peak 46,807. Source: `R2`, `SC1`

### 2017 to 2018: Fall of Oriath, consoles, China, Tencent

- **2017-01-18**: Xbox One version announced. Source: `WP-PoE`
- **2017-03-03**: **Legacy** (2.6.0): a cheap-to-build remix of past leagues to cover 3.0 development; adds Solo Self-Found; the most successful release up to then; runs 22 weeks. Steam peak 65,200. Game hook: a filler league that outperforms. Sources: `R3`, `SC1`, `PDB`
- **April 2017**: tenth anniversary profiles: 100+ staff in an old office block beside the Henderson Pak'nSave car park; 14 million people have tried the game; Wilson says roughly 10% of free-to-play players pay. Sources: `NR17`, https://thespinoff.co.nz/games/28-04-2017/grinding-gear-games-the-studio-that-prefers-to-go-with-its-gut-and-its-working/
- **2017-08-04**: **The Fall of Oriath** (3.0.0): Acts 5 to 10 replace the three-difficulty replay; more than two years in development; the decision to build six acts instead of one came halfway through. Harbinger league. Steam peak 98,110, about 50% above any earlier launch. Game hook: the big mid-project scope gamble that paid off. Sources: `R3`, `SC1`
- **2017-08-22**: open beta in **China** through Tencent, with a launch event in Shanghai. Sources: `GGG:1970529`, https://www.altchar.com/game-news/path-of-exile-is-launching-in-china-today-al8G67d7Rv1e
- **2017-08-24**: **Xbox One** release. Source: `WP-PoE`
- **2017-12-08**: **War for the Atlas** (3.1.0): Shaper versus Elder, 32 new maps; Abyss league. Built in about four months, which GGG called ambitious before vowing to return to 13 weeks. Sources: `R3`
- **2018-03-02**: **Bestiary** (3.2.0): beast capture with nets; mixed reception and heavy mid-league rework; Einhar becomes a fan favourite. First of four NPC-led leagues planned to replace the 2014 masters. Sources: `R3`, `ESC20`
- **2018-04-01**: playable battle-royale April Fools mode, "Path of Exile: Royale" `[unverified, general knowledge]`.
- **April to May 2018**: **Tencent buys a majority stake.** Three Tencent directors are appointed in April; the deal is announced 2018-05-21. Reported stake varies by source: 80% up front (NZ Herald 2018), 86.67% (Wikipedia), 87 to 88% (later NZ Herald pieces), with the remainder to follow over six years under Overseas Investment Office conditions. Price undisclosed but above the NZ$100m OIO threshold. At the time: 114 staff, 2 million+ monthly active players, 20 million+ sign-ups. Wilson says Tencent approached them and that the studio keeps operating independently. Game hook: the buy-out decision. Sources: `NZH18`, `NZH24`, `NZH25`, `WP-GGG`
- **2018-06-01**: **Incursion** (3.3.0): Alva and the Temple of Atzoatl; well liked. Steam peak 95,824. Sources: `R3`, `SC1`
- **2018-08-31**: **Delve** (3.4.0): the infinite Azurite Mine; very popular. Sources: `R3`
- **2018-11-02**: Blizzard announces Diablo Immortal at BlizzCon to a hostile crowd `[unverified date, general knowledge]`. GGG later joked that the backlash made it shelve a planned 2018 mobile announcement. Source: https://www.dexerto.com/gaming/path-of-exile-mocks-blizzard-diablo-during-mobile-announcement-1287358
- **2018-11-05**: PlayStation 4 version announced for December 2018; certification problems push it to February, then March 2019. Sources: `WP-PoE`, https://www.playstationlifestyle.net/2019/03/13/path-of-exile-ps4-release-date-revealed/

## 4. Gaps and uncertainties

1. **No Noclip documentary found.** I found no evidence that Noclip made a Path of Exile film. The closest long-form sources are PC Gamer's "The making of Path of Exile" (blocked to automated fetch; only search snippets used), GGG's own "Reflecting on Past Releases" series and "10 Years of Gears" video, and Wilson's GDC talk (not transcribed here).
2. **Near-bankruptcy anecdote.** Sources support NZ$2m spent, private investors, and pack sales bringing the studio to break-even in 2012. I did not find a direct, sourced statement that the studio was about to run out of money.
3. **Founding details conflict.** Founding year is 2006 in most sources but 2007 in Weissman's 2012 interview. Weissman counts himself a fourth co-founder; NZ sources list three. Development start is given as three or four years before the 2010 reveal. The garage was in New Lynn, not Titirangi.
4. **Henderson move date** not found (between about 2014 and April 2017).
5. **Tencent stake.** Reported as 80%, 86.67%, 87%, 87.7% and 88% for 2018 depending on the article; 93.3% by late 2023; 100% from March 2024 per Wikipedia (one blog says late 2024). The 2018 price was never disclosed beyond "over NZ$100m".
6. **Diamond pack price (US$1,000)** and the open beta / release pack price tiers are not confirmed by a fetched source. Archon (November 2026) pack prices not found.
7. **Reception column.** Only rows marked † are sourced. Other league receptions (for example Synthesis, Harvest, Sentinel, Sanctum, Crucible, Ancestor) reflect general community consensus as I know it.
8. **All-platform peaks** exist only for some launches (open beta, 1.0, Legion, Delirium, Ritual, Archnemesis, Crucible, Settlers, PoE2 early access). GGG's Crucible figure (321,180) and Settlers figure (about 350,000) may not be measured the same way. Pre-October-2013 and non-Steam numbers are otherwise missing. Steamcharts peaks run slightly below SteamDB (for example 228,398 vs 229,337).
9. **Wikipedia date errors.** Its table gives Bestiary as 2018-02-03 and Incursion as 2018-06-06; poedb and Steam data support 2018-03-02 and 2018-06-01. It also lists PoE2 0.3.0 against 2025-09-19, while the launch was 2025-08-29.
10. **Outside-event dates not re-fetched**: Diablo III launch (2012-05-15), auction house closure (2014-03-18), Diablo Immortal reveal (2018-11-02), Diablo IV launch (2023-06-06), Last Epoch 1.0 (2024-02-21), the exact Cyberpunk 2077 delay announcement, NZ lockdown dates (March 2020, August 2021). All are marked `[unverified]` in the timeline.
11. **PoE2 2020 to 2024 slips.** The "very late 2020" beta target, the 2023 split and the November-to-December 2024 delay are sourced. The 2021 gameplay showing, the exact June 2024 closed-beta date and when it was dropped, and the gamescom 2024 announcement day are not. Any promise about early access length (six months to a year) is `[unverified]`.
12. **Garena timeline** beyond the November 2013 announcement (Taiwan/Thailand/CIS launch and closure dates, Hotcool migration date) is thin.
13. **People.** Not confirmed: that Mark_GGG is Mark Roberts' handle; Rory Rackham's and Hrishi's current status; Octavian's surname; Kamil Orman-Janowski's start year; ExileCon 2019 guest list; most voice-actor credits (poewiki blocks automated access). The claim that a named person replaced Bex comes from a single blog and is left out.
14. **Chris Wilson's exit date.** His site says he stepped down at the end of 2023, yet NZ Herald quoted him as CEO in March 2024; the Companies Office date is 2025-01-21 and the director resignation is February 2025. Treat it as a gradual step-back through 2024.

16. **ExileCon 2026**: venue (Aotea Centre) and the 514,200 peak-viewer figure come from secondary sources or a headline; attendance not found. Performance claims for PoE2 1.0 conflict between outlets and are omitted.
