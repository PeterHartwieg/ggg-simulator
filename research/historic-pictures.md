# Historic pictures for the history cards

Research date: 11 November 2026. Nothing was downloaded into the project. Nothing in `art/` changed.

## Summary

- 22 entries have a strong candidate. A strong candidate is a real picture of that moment, seen on its page, with high confidence.
- 18 more entries have only a weak candidate. That means a title banner, a later photo, a press portrait or a related picture.
- 8 entries have only a text post. A screenshot of the post is the only option.
- Almost everything is GGG's own published material. None of GGG's pages state a licence, so treat it all as all rights reserved by GGG.
- Wikimedia Commons has nothing for GGG, Path of Exile, ExileCon, Chris Wilson, Jonathan Rogers, Erik Olofsson or Brian Weissman. The only freely licensed find is two CC BY-SA photos of a BlizzCon 2018 screening in Italy.
- Press photos come from NZ Herald, Stuff, The Spinoff, Newsroom and GameSpot. Each is owned by the outlet or a named photographer.

### Facts the sources contradict

The agents found these while checking dates. The card text may need a look.

- `e_payment`: GGG did not refund everyone. Its 5 April 2012 post says 7 customers were charged twice, and only their extra charges were refunded.
- `e_bex`: Bex wrote in 2019 that she started in March 2013 in customer support. She became Community Manager about February 2015.
- `e_d3`: Kripparrian's first-impressions video is dated 30 July 2012, two months after Diablo III launched.
- `e_garena`: GGG announced the deal on 5 November 2013. The press release is dated 12 November.
- `e_henderson`: GGG's forum posts place the move in 2015. In February 2015 Chris wrote that they hoped to move. By August 2015 he mentions "the new office". NZ Herald's photo caption still says Henderson in 2014.
- `e_ten`: the ten-year anniversary was November 2016. Chris announced the party on 24 October 2016.

## How to read this file

- **Owner tags.** [GGG] is GGG's own site, forum, mailers, CDN or YouTube. [PRESS] is a news outlet or its photographer. [FREE] is Creative Commons or public domain. [OTHER] is anything else, such as a creator's channel or another company.
- **Licence.** "Not stated" means the page gives no licence. Treat it as all rights reserved.
- **Seen.** "Viewed" means someone opened the image and looked at it. "Caption only" means the image tag and caption were on the page, but the file itself was not opened.
- Wayback `im_` URLs are the archive's copy of the image. The owner is still the original publisher.
